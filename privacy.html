Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Overview

YouTube Playlist Search ("the extension") does not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers.

OAuth & YouTube Data

The extension optionally uses Google OAuth to access your YouTube account. This is used solely to allow you to search your private playlists and videos. All data is fetched directly from YouTube's API and displayed in your browser — nothing is stored or transmitted elsewhere.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not store your Google account information
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking

Contact

If you have any questions, please reach out via the Chrome Web Store listing.
